Understanding the price volatility of FTC Solar

Introduction

FTC Solar were founded in 2017 and are headquartered in Austin, Texas, although they have offices in India, Australia and Spain. 2025 saw their stock price boom, although this has been followed by a recent slump in February 2026. FTC Solar produce solar tracking technology - this helps to dynamically orient solar panels in the optimal direction for maximal power generation. They also provide engineering and digital services to support installation projects.

The stock price of FTC Solar has displayed unusual short term momentum swings recently. In this data story, I try to understand what the company's fundamentals, as well as what its wider news can tell us about how investors views shift.

What's going on with FTC Solar?

FTC Solar (ticker = FTCI) is a particularly volatile solar stock. From their latest Q4 earnings report, FTC Solar's stock price has recently slumped, despite steady gains over 2025. As the chart above demonstrates, FTC Solar's stock price is characterised by large momentum swings. In this data project, I seek to understand how this uncertain past can be understood through data.

FTC Solar has accumulated liabilities

Total Shareholder Equity can be calculated as the following: Total Assets - Total Liabilities The graph above demonstrates that over time, FTC Solar has an increasing amount of liabilities and a reduction in net assets - such that liabilities have recently outweighed its total assets.

The book value of FTC Solar has plummeted...

Book value can be calculated via the following: Total Shareholder Equity Common Stock Shares Outstanding The book value theoretically estimates what a share in the company should be worth. Whilst Common Stock Shares Outstanding has steadily risen on FTC Solar's balance sheet, Total Shareholder Equity has become negative. Through the alphavantage API, I was able to plot the change in book value over the last 5 years.

Is it over-valued?

The Price:Book ratio (P/B) indicates whether a stock is over or under-valued by comparing the market price to the book value of the company.
P/B = Stock Price Book Value
FTC Solar seems to have switched from being a slightly undervalued stock to being highly overvalued. In fact, it actually reported a negative P/B ratio - indicating that it is extremely overvalued in its most recent two balance sheet reports - here represented by a arbitrary P/B value of 10. Despite the evidence pointing to a company whose liabilities are beginning to exceed its current assets, FTC Solar's stock price rose dramatically over the course of 2025.

Do the news sentiment explain the behaviour of FTC Solar?

As the scatter plot above demonstrates, there appears to be no distinct link between news sentiment and actual price movements. For my detection of news sentiment, I used the alpha vantage detected sentiment for each news article on FTC Solar for 2 years before the present day. I then plotted the subsequent time-series correlation coefficient of the FTC Solar stock price for a 24 hr window of trading following the news being released. Evidently, the alpha vantage detected sentiment of a news article is not a suitable indicator for potential price movements.
